# Raw Materials Partnership JTC 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/raw-materials-partnership-jtc-2026

The Raw Materials Partnership for the Green and Digital Transition (RAMP) is part of the Horizon Europe programme. RAMP launches the first Call for Proposals focussing on transnational projects to strengthen security of supply, circularity, and sustainability in (critical) raw material value chains. Within this call NWO makes funding available for Dutch researchers.

## Explore

### Purpose

Raw materials are essential for the green and digital transition. They are needed in technologies such as batteries, renewable energy systems and advanced manufacturing. The growing demand, vulnerable supply chains and geopolitical uncertainty have increased the need for sustainable, resilient and circular raw materials systems.

RAMP expects proposals which focus on improving access to raw materials while promoting circular economy practices, achieving sustainability, and minimising social and environmental costs. Proposals are encouraged to connect the different steps of the supply chain, bridge multiple disciplines, engage with stakeholders and situate themselves in the relevant policy context.

For Dutch researchers, the Call will be open for proposals relating to one of the following topics:

- Topic 4: Overcoming technological barriers to circularity
- Topic 5: Putting circular economy ambitions into action
- Topic 6: Innovative approaches for analysing raw materials value chains

### Who can apply

This Call is open to transnational consortia of at least three partners requesting funding from three participating countries. At least two of these must be EU Member States or associated countries. Organisations which are not eligible for funding from a participating funder may join a consortium as self-funded partners, in addition to the minimum composition of the consortium.

On the RAMP website, you can find a list of participating countries and search for partners using the partner search tool.

Researchers from Dutch universities, universities of applied sciences and other knowledge institutions can submit applications to NWO. See the national requirements for NOW and the Call for Proposals for further information.

An application for funding from NWO may consider the following roles:

- National main applicant (mandatory): the national applicant who leads the application to NWO and is the foreseen national project leader, in case a project is granted.
- National co-applicant (optional): national applicants in addition to the national main applicant with an active role and responsibility in realising the project and requesting funding from NWO.
A researcher may only request NWO funding for one project (part of a European consortium) in this Call for proposals, either as national main applicant or national co-applicant within an application for NWO funding.

### What to apply for

NWO has made available 2 million euro in this Call.

A maximum of 400,000 euro can be applied for at NWO for a project proposal in this Call. The maximum project duration is three years. Available NWO budget modules are: Personnel (Postdoc, Non-Scientific Personnel, Research leave), Material costs, Knowledge utilisation and Project management. Please refer to the document ‘Explanation of budget modules and indexing’ for further details.

### Assessment

### Criteria

Proposals will be assessed comparatively in terms of their

- Excellence
- Impact
- Implementation efficiency (only in stage 2, full proposal)
A full description of the assessment criteria can be found in the Call for proposals.

### Procedure

The assessment in this call will be done in two stages. In the first stage, consortia are invited to submit a pre-proposal. In the second stage selected consortia are invited to submit a full proposal. Eligible proposals both in the first and in the second stage will be assessed by an independent evaluation committee.

The funding partners in this call will subsequently make a selection for either stage 1 or stage 2 based on the assessment and the available budgets. After the transnational selection of proposals in the second stage, NWO’s Executive Board will take the finale decision regarding projects funded by NWO.
