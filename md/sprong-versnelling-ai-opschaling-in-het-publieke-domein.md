# SPRONG-versnelling: AI- opschaling in het publieke domein

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/sprong-versnelling-ai-opschaling-in-het-publieke-domein

The SPRONG-versnelling: AI- opschaling in het publieke domein brings universities of applied sciences together in a broad research group. Together with practice partners, they form a consortium that works on making AI applications responsible and scalable, among other things by developing and sharing knowledge on scaling methods, implementation, and governance. By investing in knowledge infrastructure and collaboration, this consortium will grow over four years into a national knowledge infrastructure that accelerates AI scaling and contributes to a future-proof public sector.
