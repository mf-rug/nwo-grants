# Metal Power - Systemic and societal challenges

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/metal-power-systemic-and-societal-challenges

The energy transition calls for innovative solutions that are not only sustainable, but also scalable, safe and economically viable. A promising technology in this domain focuses on the use of metal powders as a renewable energy carrier. During oxidation (combustion) of metal powders no CO2 is released by the reaction itself. Moreover, the resulting products (metal oxides) can be largely converted back into metal powders, for example by using green electricity or green hydrogen. This new technology therefore offers a promising solution for industry and many other applications. This call focuses on the systemic and societal challenges that hinder large-scale use of metal powders and encourages the development of transdisciplinary solutions.
