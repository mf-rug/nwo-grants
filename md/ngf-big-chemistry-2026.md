# NGF - Big Chemistry 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/ngf-big-chemistry-2026

This Call for proposals focuses on predicting the physical and chemical properties of complex molecular systems using AI. This Call for proposals thus aims to bridge experimental chemistry, automation and AI by integrating technological reaction platforms for parallel and/or serial experiments, robotic tools for lab automation and the development of chemistry-specific AI models.

More specifically defined, projects submitted to this Call for proposals should serve one or more of the three research lines listed below, and should contain both a chemistry and an AI component:

- Foams and emulsions

- Coatings, adhesives, inks

- Drug-delivery systems
