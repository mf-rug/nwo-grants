# Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership (SBEP) – Call 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/sustainable-blue-economy-partnership-sbep-call-2026

The Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership (SBEP) is a European partnership under Horizon Europe. Its aim is to stimulate and accelerate the transition to a climate-neutral, sustainable and productive Blue Economy in the North Sea, Baltic Sea, Mediterranean Sea, Black Sea and the Atlantic Ocean.

The partners in SBEP are jointly launching a transnational call for proposals, under which NWO intends to make funding available to applicants in the Kingdom of the Netherlands. NWO intends to make funding available for the following priority areas 1) Transitioning the blue economy sectors for sustainability and strategic autonomy 2) Managing sea-uses by advancing digital and ecosystem-based approaches 3) Promoting sustainable blue bioresources through innovation in marine production and harvesting 4) Building resilience in coastal communities with infrastructure and businesses

Final confirmation of participation in call modules and the national conditions will be provided upon launch of the call for proposals in mid-September at the latest. Up-to-date information is available on the SBEP website [https://www.bluepartnership.eu/funding-opportunity/preannouncement-sustainable-blue-economy-partnerships-fourth-joint ]
