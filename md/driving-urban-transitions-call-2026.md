# Driving Urban Transitions Call 2026​

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/driving-urban-transitions-call-2026

​​Driving Urban Transitions to a sustainable future (DUTP) is a European Partnership under Horizon Europe. he aim of the partnership is to create attractive, sustainable and economically viable urban areas in which European citizens, communities and their environment thrive.

## Characteristics

**Status:** In preparation  
**Closing date for pre-proposals:** 17 November 2026 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)  
**Closing date full application:** 15 April 2027 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)

## Explore

​​The research and innovation funders in DUTP are jointly launching a transnational Call for proposals, in which NWO intends to make funding available for researchers in the Kingdom of the Netherlands. NWO expects to open themes for research oriented projects on the urban energy transition, mobility and circular economy. The definitive participation in themes and the national requirements will, at the latest, be confirmed with the launch of the Call for proposals. Current information is available on the [DUTP-website](https://eur05.safelinks.protection.outlook.com/?url=https%3A%2F%2Fdutpartnership.eu%2F&data=05%7C02%7CEP%40NWO.NL%7C2969a6bbebe64da7b17908ded8d50bd1%7C81e63bdd534e4aaf855a4c017eec7126%7C0%7C0%7C639186606700517738%7CUnknown%7CTWFpbGZsb3d8eyJFbXB0eU1hcGkiOnRydWUsIlYiOiIwLjAuMDAwMCIsIlAiOiJXaW4zMiIsIkFOIjoiTWFpbCIsIldUIjoyfQ%3D%3D%7C0%7C%7C%7C&sdata=k75rGUkfBzq7V3NfSQOBLE5shkjFQxrTEmfpMrWqpBk%3D&reserved=0). 
 
NWO intends to open its application portal within the DUT Call 2026 for proposals covering the six topics listed below.

​15mC  topic 2 - Align ambitions for mobility transitions across sectors  
15mC topic 3 - Nurture neighbourhood economies – activate innovation in local everyday offers  
 
CUE topic 2 - Innovative maintenance of urban green and blue spaces  
CUE topic 3 -  Rethinking urban production for circular and resilient cities  
 
PED topic 2 - PED solutions in private housing across different urban contexts 
PED topic 3 - Sustainable PEDs, flexibility in energy systems, and integrated technologies​
