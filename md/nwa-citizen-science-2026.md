# NWA Citizen Science 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/nwa-citizen-science-2026

With this Call for Proposals, NWO aims to strengthen collaboration between citizens and researchers within the Routes of the National Research Agenda. Through this instrument, NWO aims to offer engaged and curious citizens, organized into a citizen collective, the opportunity to address a societal issue in collaboration with researchers and within the framework of the National Science Agenda routes. By combining their experience and unique insights into the issue with the scientific insights of researchers, they can work together to develop potential solutions to the issue.

## Explore

### Purpose

Through this Call for Proposals, NWO aims to strengthen collaboration between citizens and researchers within the Routes of the Dutch Research Agenda. Through this instrument, NWO aims to offer engaged and inquisitive citizens, brought together in a citizens’ collective, the opportunity to address a societal issue in collaboration with researchers and within the framework of the Dutch Science Agenda routes. By combining their experience and unique insight into the issue with the scientific insights of researchers, they can work together to develop potential solutions to the issue.

### Who can apply

Applications must be submitted by a lead applicant on behalf of the consortium. Under this Call for Proposals, applications will only be considered if they are submitted via one of the 25 NWA routes. To this end, when submitting the application, the applicant must provide the ‘Confirmation of NWA Route Affiliation’ statement, in which the route’s management confirms that the application is being submitted on their behalf. A template for the ‘Confirmation of Route Affiliation’ statement has been made available by NWO on the funding page of this Call for proposals.

A maximum of two applications per NWA route may be considered. The selection of these applications is the responsibility of the routes.

Four categories of consortium participants are distinguished:

- lead applicant;
- co-applicant(s);
- collaboration partner(s);
- citizen collectives.
A consortium must consist of at least one lead applicant and one citizens’ collective. The conditions for each participant are explained in more detail in the following sections.

### Consortium building

To increase the likelihood of the research having an impact, collaboration between researchers and partners from civil society is required. In the Call for Proposals, NWO supports the formation of a consortium by and with citizen collectives, in which the research question originates from these collectives. Work is carried out jointly on drafting the application and conducting the research via and within the 25 NWA routes. This methodology is explained in more detail in Appendix 7.4.

### What to apply for

The maximum grant amount per project is €100,000. It is expected that up to 25 applications will be awarded in this round. Under this Call for Proposals, applications are put forward via the 25 NWA routes, with the research questions being formulated by a citizens’ collective. In principle, one application will be awarded per NWA route (see Chapter 4). If one or more applications do not meet the set quality requirements, NWO may proceed to award a second application put forward by one of the other routes. This decision will be made on the basis of the priorities established by the assessment committee.

### Assessment

### Criteria

The assessment is based on substantive evaluation criteria and begins once an application has been accepted for consideration. Applications are assessed on their merits using the following evaluation criteria:

- Problem definition and analysis (30%)
- Expected impact and pathway to impact (20%)
- Quality of collaboration (30%)
- Quality of the research (20%)

### Procedure

The application assessment procedure consists of the following steps (see the timeline in section 3.1):

- preliminary assessment by the assessment committee;
- meeting of the assessment committee;
- decision-making.
For this round, an external, independent assessment committee will be appointed by the NWO Executive Board. Its members will be drawn from the scientific community and the entire knowledge chain, including societal stakeholders and representatives from the target group, with expertise in the relevant field. The task of the assessment committee is to assess the submitted applications against the assessment criteria. Each application is assessed on its own merits.

Applications are assessed by an assessment committee, without reference opinions.
