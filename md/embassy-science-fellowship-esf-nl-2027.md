# Embassy Science Fellowship (ESF-NL) 2027

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/embassy-science-fellowship-esf-nl-2027

The ESF-NL programme aims to strengthen the interaction between diplomacy and science by placing fellows within missions. Acting as a bridge between the scientific and diplomatic domains, fellows apply their scientific expertise and international networks to inform and support evidence-informed Dutch foreign policy. In doing so, they contribute to strengthening scientific cooperation with third countries while gaining practical experience in a diplomatic context.

### Purpose

For the Embassy Science Fellows Programme (ESF-NL), the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and NWO are looking for 12 researchers who would like to bring their scientific expertise and networks to a mission in Australia, Canada, Chili, Egypt, Ivory Coast, Malaysia, PV Rome, Senegal, South-Africa, South-Korea, Taiwan or Vietnam for a period of 1 to 3 months. The project descriptions per mission are found in the Call for Proposals annexes.

ESF-NL offers missions access to scientific and technological expertise. Researchers from universities and other Dutch knowledge institutions can contribute to activities that strengthen scientific and diplomatic relations between the Netherlands and the host mission. For example, Embassy Science Fellows can be sent to meet foreign researchers, give advice, or carry out studies or produce technical reports.

As such, the ESF-NL aims to achieve cross-fertilisation between diplomacy and science. Fellows can gain experience in the diplomatic world and use their expertise to inform Dutch foreign policy. The fellows give an additional boost to the scientific cooperation with third countries and feed the policies of missions with scientific knowledge.

Researchers can apply for a maximum of 1 of the 12 selected positions for 2027. The selected fellows will be posted for 1 to 3 months. The mission will help fellows to find accommodation and a place to work.

### Who can apply

This Call for proposals is open to all PhD graduates at any of the following research organisations and to 'lectoren' and senior researchers of universities of applied sciences:

- Universities and universities of applied sciences, as described in Section 1.8, subsection 1 of the Dutch Higher Education and Research Act, or universities referred to in the Policy Rules for Universities in the Kingdom of the Netherlands;
- university medical centres, as described in Section 1.13, subsection 1 of the Higher Education and Research Act;
- KNAW institutes and NWO institutesKNAW institutesNWO institutes;
- Netherlands Cancer Institute;
- Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics in Nijmegen;
- Naturalis Biodiversity Center;
- Advanced Research Centre for NanoLithography (ARCNL);
- Princess Máxima Center;
- IHE Delft Institute for Water Education;
- TO2 institutions.
The contract of the applicant should be running at least until the end of the fellowship period. Researchers who did not obtain their PhD degree yet, but whose PhD date is fixed are also eligible to submit a proposal. However, they must have obtained their PhD degree at the start of the fellowship.

### What to apply for

The available budget for this Call for proposals is €390,000. It is possible to apply for a grant with a maximum of €30,000. The minimum duration of the proposed fellowship is 1 month, the maximum duration of the proposed fellowship is 3 months. The duration of the fellowship does not necessarily need to be continuous, i.e., fellows can choose to schedule their 1 to 3 month stay intermittently, over a longer period of time, but the travel frequency is restricted to maximum of 2 return trips.
Fellows can budget for:

1. International travel expenses for economy class air travel or train fares;
2. Accommodation expenses;
3. Research expenses, including domestic travel, representation expenses, costs for meetings and other costs necessary to carry out the research;
4. Replacement grant (See CfP for more information).

### Assessment

### Criteria

Proposals are substantively assessed against the following criteria:

1. Quality and motivation of the applicant (40% weighting in assessment)1. Scientific quality and experience, as demonstrated from the narrative CV, including key results, scientific outputs and/or other scientific achievements;
2. Evidence of capacity for cooperation and independence;
3. Ability to communicate the benefits of scientific research to potential knowledge users;
4. Motivation of the applicant to contribute to the objective of the ESF-NL programme.
2. Match between the applicant and the assignment as formulated by the mission (40% weighting in assessment)1. Clear match between the applicant’s profile and the fellowship, or a clear vision of how this match will be achieved;
2. Experience with the topic as formulated in the fellowship and with the mission in question.
3. Feasibility (20% weighting in assessment)
The applicant can be posted abroad to the mission for 1-3 months, and the proposed assignment can be carried out within the proposed time frame.

### Procedure

The assessment procedure for a proposal consists of the following steps (see timeline):

- assessment committee meeting;
- decision-making.
Given the expertise present in the assessment committee and the small size of the grant, NWO has opted to apply the provision in Article 2.2.4, second subsection of the NWO Grant Rules 2024 for assessment of proposals without involving referees.

### Write proposal

The application takes the form of a motivation letter:

- Motivation letter (PFD, max. 2 x A4 in Calibri Font, Size 10 with normal margins and single line spacing), including a provisional working plan detailing your goals, objectives and action steps. Please state in your motivation in which fellowship you are interested, why you are suitable for the fellowship, why your fellowship will be of value (what will you bring), your availability in the period 1 March 2027 to 29 February 2028, any experience with the subject and possibly with the mission in question, and your approach to involving relevant local partners/stakeholders in the research;
You are also requested to include a narrative Curriculum Vitae (CV) and key outputs:

- CV (PDF, max. 500 words); provide a comprehensive narrative of your scientific achievements, research focus, research agenda, position in your (inter)national scientific field, motivation, and the scientific and societal potential of your work.
- Key outputs (PDF, max. 5 items); please include references to your key outputs. Output may include, but is not limited to refereed articles, non-refereed articles, letters (to editors), books, book chapters, pre-prints, patents, working papers, proceedings, conference reports, software, code and open access databases. You may mention all types of output that occur in your field. The status of your output must be clearly indicated. You are allowed to use one hyperlink per item, which refers directly to the output (e.g., a DOI). You may not mention h-indexes, journal impact factors, or any other indicator or term that refers to the general quality or reputation of a journal, publisher, or publication platform, rather than to the individual output item.
The proposal is written in English. It is mandatory to upload the following annexes to the proposal in ISAAC:

- budget (Excel file) (standard format provided).
