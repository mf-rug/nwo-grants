# SSH Open Competition Explore 2027 – round 1

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/ssh-open-competition-explore-2027-round-1

The SSH Open Competition Explore is intended for researchers who wish to investigate a groundbreaking idea in a relatively small-scale, rapidly launchable fundamental research project within the disciplines of the NWO-domain Social Sciences and Humanities. Thanks to a short turnaround time between proposal and commencement, researchers can respond quickly to new opportunities, current developments or insights. Applicants assess each other’s proposal through anonymous distributed peer review. 

SSH Open Competition Explore is the new name of the former SSH Open Competition XS programme. The Call for proposals of 2027 has 3 rounds. This information concerns the first round.

## Explore

### Purpose

Some research ideas are so innovative that they warrant investigation, even if the outcomes are still uncertain. These may include an innovative idea, a new method, an experimental approach or an ambitious research objective whose feasibility has not yet been established. NWO is seeking initiatives of this kind within the SSH Open Competition Explore. Well-developed ideas that build on previous insights also fit within Explore, provided the research is innovative in nature.

Explore offers scope to launch a new line of research, develop a method, carry out an initial empirical test, build on existing knowledge or lay the foundations for future collaboration. The research contributes to new and existing scientific insights and may also focus on societal impact. Precisely because this is pioneering research, it is not necessary to establish in advance that the intended results will be achieved. Unexpected outcomes can also yield valuable knowledge.

### Who can apply

Researchers holding a PhD may submit a proposal provided they hold a paid position for at least the duration of the application process and the duration of the proposed project at one of research organisations mentioned in section 3.2 of the Call for proposals. Individuals with a zero-hour employment contract cannot submit a proposal.

Additional conditions:

- an Explore proposal has a single applicant and no co-applicants;
- an applicant may not have already submitted a proposal in the same calendar year (2027) as a lead or co-applicant within the NWO/ZonMw Open Competition programme;
- at the time of submission, applicants may not have an ongoing NWO/ZonMw Open Competition XS or Explore project for which the end date has not yet passed;
- principal and co-applicants for an NWO/ZonMw Open Competition project, other than an Open Competition XS/Explore project, may submit a proposal no earlier than one year before the planned end date (ISAAC) of their project.

### What to apply for

Per project, it is possible to apply for a grant with a maximum of €50,000. The minimum duration of the proposed project is 6 months, and the maximum duration is 24 months. You may claim costs for personnel and materials. Request only the amounts of funding necessary to complete the project. A more detailed explanation of the budget modules can be found in Annex 7.1 to this Call for proposals.
Co-financing is not allowed. An additional contribution (financial or in-kind) from the research organisation is possible, but not mandatory. If this is done, a statement exceeding maximum funding must be submitted.

NWO will only reimburse project costs that are not or will not be financed from other sources. No double funding is permitted.

### When to apply

### Assessment

### Criteria

Peer review is carried out by applicants on the basis of the information provided in the application form, using an anonymous distributed peer review process.
Proposals must be written in a manner that is sufficiently accessible to a broad scientific audience, so that they can be assessed by assessors from different disciplinary backgrounds. A proposal that is not written in a sufficiently accessible manner may be given a lower priority by a assessor for that reason.

In order to perform the ranking of the proposals (see also section 4.2.2 in the Call for proposals), the assessor considers the substantive assessment criteria set out below. No weighting has been assigned to these criteria. Both criteria may be used at the assessor’s discretion to determine the ranking. However, this ranking must be accompanied by a brief written explanation substantiating the ranking on the basis of the criteria set out below:

#### Innovation and dissemination

Is the proposed research innovative and groundbreaking? Does the proposed research contribute to existing and new scientific insights? Is there an appropriate strategy for disseminating the research results within the own and the broader scientific community, and, if applicable, to society?

#### Research plan

Are the objective and research plan clearly described? Is the methodology suitable for achieving the stated project goal? Is the timetable for the research plan appropriate, and are the activities set out clearly enough in the planning? Is the expertise of the research team appropriate for the proposed research?

### Procedure

For this Call for proposals, the broad field of SSH has been divided into three panels, each comprising a combination of SSH disciplines. The applicant determines by which panel the application will be processed by means of the information form (see Section 4.2.1 of the Call for proposals). For each panel, proposals taken into consideration are divided into two groups, meaning that each panel consists of Group A and Group B. In principle, the allocation to the groups is based on the date of submission to NWO, taking into account the personal interests specified by the applicants.

The assessment is carried out via anonymous distributed peer review by the applicants themselves. NWO determines the final ranking of the proposals on the basis of the ranking scores received (see section 4.2.3 of the Call for proposals). A maximum of six final rankings are generated per round; for each panel, one for group A and one for group B. 
The assessors and applicants of a round do not meet for consultation. There is no opportunity for a rebuttal.

The procedures followed are assessed by the NWO Domain Board SSH. Decisions on the approval or rejection of proposals are made on the basis of the final rankings and the available funding. Following this decision, NWO informs the applicant of their proposal’s award or rejection.

### Write proposal

Proposals must be written in a manner that is sufficiently accessible to a broad scientific audience, so that they can be assessed by assessors from different disciplinary backgrounds.

Applicants must present their research idea anonymously in the application form.

In practical terms, this means that the application form must meet the following conditions:

- applicants should not include their own name nor the name of any individuals in their research group;
- applicants should not include details of any affiliations with institutes or knowledge institutions;
- applicants should not include references to any publications that they have authored or co-authored;
- applicants should not include the names of any partners (or potential partners);
- applicants do not mention any other information on the basis of which they can be identified.
