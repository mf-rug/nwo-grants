# NGF CIIIC Learning Communities for IX-talent and skills

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/ngf-ciiic-learning-communities-for-ix-talent-and-skills

This Call for Proposals focuses on the development of learning communities that contribute to increasing the pool of knowledge and talent for CIIIC, as well as enriching the knowledge, skills, and competencies of current professionals. The total available budget amounts to €10,800,000. Per project, a minimum of €900,000 and a maximum of €1,800,000 in funding can be requested, for a maximum project duration of 48 months.

## Characteristics

**Status:** Open calls Open for applications  
**Closing date full application:** 3 November 2026 14:00 hrs.
