# Gravitation 2027 - Frontier research for consortia

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/gravitation-2027-frontier-research-for-consortia

Gravitation is intended for consortia of leading researchers who wish to collaborate over the long term on innovative, world-class research that sustainably strengthens the Netherlands’ position in science.

## Explore

### Purpose

The Gravitation programme brings together researchers from one or more disciplines into consortia capable of positioning themselves at the global forefront of their respective research fields.

### Who can apply

Only the Executive Boards of universities based in the Kingdom of the Netherlands —as the primary recipients of funding from the Ministry of Education, Culture and Science (OCW) (first stream funding)— may submit proposals to NWO under this programme. Each Executive Board may submit a maximum of four proposals. The technical submission via ISAAC must be carried out by the consortium coordinator. The coordinator serves as the primary point of contact for the proposal and, together with up to five other ‘principal investigators’ (PIs), forms the consortium’s scientific leadership.

In this Call for proposals, the coordinator and co-PIs are referred to as the ‘main applicant’ and ‘co-applicants’, respectively.

### What to apply for

The total budget available for this call for proposals is €159,250,000. A maximum of €22,750,000 and a minimum of €10,000,000 may be requested per project. The proposed project has a duration of ten years. The main applicant and co-applicants may request funding for personnel costs, equipment, investments and knowledge utilisation. The available budget modules, including explanatory notes and rates, are set out in the Call for proposals.

### When to apply

- The deadline for submitting the proposals is **30 March 2027, before 14:00:00 CET**.

### Assessment

### Criteria

The assessment will be based on the following criteria:

1. Quality of the researchers involved and of the consortium (30% weighting)
2. Quality of the scientific research programme (30% weighting)
3. Organisational structure and governance of the consortium; budget (20% weighting)
4. Scientific and/or societal impact (20% weighting)

### Procedure

After submission via ISAAC, NWO will first check whether the proposal meets the formal submission requirements. The proposal will then be assessed in writing by external referees, with the consortium given an opportunity to respond. The proposal, referee reports and response will subsequently be assessed by members of the assessment committee. The most promising consortia will be invited for an interview.

Based on the written information and the interview, the assessment committee will establish a ranking of the proposals and advise the NWO Executive Board on whether proposals should be awarded or rejected. The NWO Executive Board will review the procedure followed, decide on the award or rejection of proposals, and determine the final qualifications.

## Prepare

Proposals must be submitted through the ISAAC proposal and reporting platform. If you do not have an ISAAC account yet, then please create one at least one week in advance. More information about writing a proposal and which forms you need to use can be found in ISAAC. Please download and use the forms in ISAAC. Below you will find examples of the required forms.

Start your submission in ISAAC at least one week before the submission deadline. You can save your progress and complete the submission later. Please note that you will need to enter additional information in the system, including the [NWO research fields](https://www.nwo.nl/en/nwo-research-fields) and a public summary in both Dutch and English.

### Fill in forms

Include the following mandatory annexes with your application by uploading them in ISAAC:

- a covering letter from the submitting institution, in English;
- a budget (Excel file);
- a knowledge security statement (if applicable; see Section 4.3.5);
- a co-financing statement (if applicable; see Section 3.4.1);
- an appointment and project supervision statement (PDF) (if applicable).
Use only the templates provided by NWO for these annexes.

No annexes other than those listed above are permitted. With the exception of the budget, all annexes must be submitted as unsecured PDF files. The budget must be submitted as an Excel file in ISAAC.
