# RAAK-mkb September 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/raak-mkb-september-2026

The RAAK mkb scheme promotes collaboration between universities of applied sciences and small and medium sized enterprises (SMEs). Networks of universities of applied sciences, research organizations, and SMEs conduct practice oriented research driven by questions from professional practice. The results of this research provide practically applicable knowledge for professionals working in SMEs.
