# Foundations for climate-resilient cultural heritage (RCH JTC 2027)

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/foundations-for-climate-resilient-cultural-heritage-rch-jtc-2027

The European Partnership Resilient Cultural Heritage invites researchers and societal partners to form a transnational consortium for research to climate-resilient cultural heritage: assessment and management of impact and risks of climate change to cultural heritage, and dealing with damage to and loss of cultural heritage as a result of climate change. For this call, NWO makes funding available for researchers in the Kingdom of the Netherlands, as part of transnational consortia of at least three countries.

## Explore

### Purpose

The European Partnership Resilient Cultural Heritage (RCH) is a strategic collaboration between over 90 organisations and the European Commission, with the shared aim of strengthening the resilience of cultural heritage against climate change and enhancing its contribution to climate adaptation.

This first Call for proposals by RCH aims to support projects that are based on the experiences of stakeholders and that build on existing research and initiatives. Proposals must incorporate scientific research, innovative technological solutions and community-led approaches. The focus is on integrated risk assessment methodologies that serve as a basis for long-term planning for societal resilience, the management and governance of cultural heritage, and climate measures adapted to cultural heritage.

This Call for proposals focusses on four sub-themes:

1. Risk and impact assessment and cultural heritage risk preparedness and management;
2. Managing loss and damage of cultural heritage impacted by climate change-induced natural hazards;
3. Traditional, local and Indigenous knowledge systems and practices as resources and frameworks for societal resilience in the context of climate change;
4. Sustainable governance of the cultural heritage sector.

### Who can apply

This call is open to transnational consortia of at least three partners from three different participating countries requesting funding from at least three different funders. Organisations which are not eligible for funding from a participating funder may join a consortium as self-funded partner.

In the Call for proposals, you can find a list of participating countries.

Researchers from research organisations in the Kingdom of the Netherlands can submit applications for funding from NWO. See the national requirements for NWO and the Call for Proposals for further information.

Please note: for an NWO proposal, the following roles are available:

- National main applicant (mandatory): the national applicant who leads the application for funding from NWO and is the foreseen national project leader, in case a project is granted;
- National co-applicant (optional): national applicant in addition to the national main applicant with an active role and responsibility in realising the project and requesting funding from NWO;
A researcher may only request NWO funding for one project (part of a European consortium) in this Call for proposals, either as national main applicant or national co-applicant within an application for NWO funding.

### What to apply for

A maximum of 400,000 euro can be applied for from NWO for a project proposal in this call. The maximum project duration is three years. Available NWO budget modules are: Personnel, Material costs, Knowledge utilisation and Project management. Please refer to the document ‘Explanation of budget modules and indexing’ for further details.

### When to apply

- The deadline for submitting the pre-proposal (stage 1 - obligatory) is **11 January 2027 before 14:00:00 CET**.
- The deadline for submitting the full proposal (stage 2 - on invitation) is **16 June 2027 before 14:00:00 CEST**.
**Note:** If your organisation must be assessed by NWO in order to participate as a national co-applicant, additional deadlines apply prior to the submission deadlines stated above. See the National Annex for further information.

### Assessment

### Criteria

Proposals will be assessed based on:

1. Excellence
2. Impact
3. Quality and efficiency of the implementation
A full description of the assessment criteria can be found in the Call for proposals. Applicants are advised that the (sub-)criteria differ between phases.

### Procedure

The assessment in this call will be done in two stages. In the first stage, consortia are invited to submit a preproposal. In the second stage selected consortia are invited to submit a full proposal. Eligible proposals both in the first and in the second stage will be assessed by an independent evaluation committee.

The funders in this Call will subsequently make a selection for either stage 1 or stage 2 based on the assessment and the available budgets. After the transnational selection of proposals in the second stage, NWO’s Executive Board will take the final decision regarding projects funded by NWO.
