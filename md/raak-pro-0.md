# RAAK-PRO

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/raak-pro-0

Lectors and researchers, together with the knowledge network and professional practice, conduct practice-oriented research for four years. This creates a sustainable collaborative relationship, promotes the quality of practice-oriented research, and strengthens the research capacity at the university of applied sciences. RAAK-PRO helps universities of applied sciences with their profiling agenda and to connect with economic and social developments.
