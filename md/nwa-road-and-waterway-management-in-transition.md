# NWA Road and Waterway Management in Transition

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/nwa-road-and-waterway-management-in-transition

The Dutch transport infrastructure faces a major modernisation challenge, with capacity and the availability of expertise coming under increasing pressure. System solutions are therefore needed for a new infrastructure and asset management system that is suited to the challenges and requirements of our time. A learning-based approach to collaboration must be central to this system. The NWA ‘Joint programming’ programme ‘Road and Waterway Management in Transition’, uses living labs to investigate specific maintenance tasks in the context of North Holland. These insights are consolidated within an overarching project to develop integrated knowledge and practical approaches for waterway and road managers and public authorities. In this way, the programme contributes to a smart, sustainable and efficient system for the management and maintenance of, amongst other things, roads, waterways, bridges and lock gates.
