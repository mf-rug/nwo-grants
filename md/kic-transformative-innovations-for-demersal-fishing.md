# KIC- Transformative innovations for demersal fishing

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/kic-transformative-innovations-for-demersal-fishing

Fisheries play an important social role in providing sufficient, safe and high-quality food. To maintain and strengthen a healthy demersal fisheries sector, groundbreaking innovations within bottom-trawling fisheries are essential. This Call for proposals, in collaboration with the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, Food Security and Nature, encourages research that yields new knowledge and insights enabling the development of transformative innovations for bottom-trawling fisheries. This encompasses innovations relating to reduced emissions and reduced seabed disturbance, as well as responsible economic management.
