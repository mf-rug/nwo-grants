Are you working with your network on the climate transition and looking for financial support to get your network up and running or further professionalise it? Or do you need support to set up a transdisciplinary climate transition network? Our network grants can help you move ahead.

## Explore

### Purpose

The aim of KIN Pact Network Grants is to provide financial support for setting up and professionalising transdisciplinary climate transition networks. This applies both to existing networks and to networks that have yet to start. KIN’s aim is to foster collaboration between diverse stakeholders across a range of disciplines and help professionalise the use of knowledge for climate transition themes. One prerequisite for receiving a grant is that existing or yet to be built networks become members of the KIN Pact. You can read more about the requirements in section 3. KIN Pact Network Grants are emphatically not intended for networks whose focus is strictly on a single field of expertise (monodisciplinary) or restricted to conducting research. Transdisciplinarity is understood to mean that the network consists of more than one subject discipline, as well as of more than one type of organisation (knowledge institution, civil society organisation, government body, business, etc.).

### Who can apply

Employees of any legal entity registered in the Kingdom of the Netherlands are allowed to submit an application.

One prerequisite for receiving a grant is that main and co-applicants representing existing or yet to be built networks are members of the KIN Pact at the time they submit their proposal.

- Main and co-applicants include at least one knowledge institution (i.e. organisations that conduct research, such as higher education institutions, scientific and applied research institutions, national government institutes and university medical centres), plus at least two civil society organisations (i.e. organisations that are not knowledge institutions, government bodies or businesses and serve the general interests of society). The network may include additional members who are not part of the proposal.
- The network must be transdisciplinary; this means that it brings together experts from a range of disciplines, different types of organisations and a variety of interest groups to form a single network.

### What to apply for

A maximum of €30,000 in funding can be requested per application. Each network can submit one application. The grant must be used within one year of receipt; this does not coincide with the duration of the network.

### Assessment

## Criteria

1. Alignment with the KIN and KIN Pact mission (weighting: 50%)
2. Suitability of activities and budget (weighting: 50%)

## Procedure

The application procedure consists of the following steps:

- submission of the proposal;
- review of the proposal;
- assessment;
- decision-making.
