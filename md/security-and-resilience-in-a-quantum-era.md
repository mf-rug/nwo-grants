# Security and Resilience in a Quantum Era

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/security-and-resilience-in-a-quantum-era

Researchers are invited to submit proposals for research on portable entanglement in quantum networks and post-quantum cryptography. The focus is on fundamental research, with further development and long-term applications in defence-relevant contexts in mind. Proposals may be submitted individually or on behalf of a consortium.

## Explore

### Purpose

The aim of this research programme is to strengthen a future-proof, sustainable knowledge base in the field of quantum technology. This Call for Proposals distinguishes between two lines of research:

- **Research line 1: Portable entanglement for quantum networking applications**. The aim of this research line is to investigate pathways towards making entanglement ‘portable’: i.e. entanglement is generated at one location (which may be via fibre-optics networks or satellites) and then stored in a portable memory for extended periods of time (e.g. a day).
- **Research line 2: Post-quantum cryptography (PQC)**. This line of research aims to increase our knowledge and confidence in the security of existing and new cryptographic alg rithms, schemes and protocols. These techniques must be resistant to quantum attacks, but can be implemented on conventional ICT systems without the need for quantum hardware.

### Who can apply

Proposals may be submitted individually by a single main applicant (for both research lines) or on behalf of a consortium (research line 1 only).

There are four categories of participants within a consortium:

1. main applicant;
2. co-applicant(s);
3. cooperation partner(s);
4. co-funder(s).
The terms and conditions for each participant are detailed in the Call for proposals.

### What to apply for

The following funding may be applied for under each research line:

- **Research line 1: Portable entanglement for quantum networking applications**. A maximum grant of €1,000,000 may be applied for per project if the application is submitted by a single main applicant. If the application is submitted by a consortium, a grant of between €1,000,000 and €1,500,000 may be applied for per project.
- **Research line 2: Post-quantum cryptography (PQC)**. A maximum grant of €385,000 may be applied for per project. Under this research strand, applications may only be submitted by a single main applicant. The funding must be used to fund at least one temporary academic post (PhD student or postdoc) in combination with the other available budget modules. For both research lines, the maximum duration of the proposed project is 5 years. Within both research lines, costs may be claimed for personnel, materials, investments, and knowledge utilisation.

### Assessment

### Criteria

- Criterion 1: Fit with the programme’s thematic description (accounts for 35% of the assessment);
- Criterion 2: Scientific quality of the proposal (accounts for 35% of the assessment);
- Criterion 3: Scientific and/or societal impact (accounts for 10% of the assessment);
- Criterion 4: Quality of the consortium (applicable to research line 1) or the researcher (applicable to research line 1 with only a main applicant and research line 2) (accounts for 20% of the assessment).

### Procedure

The substantive assessment is carried out by an assessment committee, without the involvement of external referees. If the budget request is three times the available funding, an interview selection process may take place. The lead applicant will then be invited to an interview with the assessment committee. Based on both the written application and the interview, the assessment committee will make a recommendation to the NWO Executive Board. The NWO Executive Board will decide on the submitted proposals. The main applicant will be notified whether the application has been awarded funding.

### Write proposal

The steps involved in writing your proposal are:

- create an account or update your existing account details if necessary;
- use ISAAC to download the proposal form and required formats for annexes;
- save the completed proposal form as a PDF file and submit it in ISAAC. Submit any annexes separately;
- in ISAAC, fill in all the requested information, including the re search fields [NWO research fields | NWO](/en/nwo-research-fields) and a summary for non-experts in Dutch and English;
- if you need to register an organisation that isn’t yet included in the ISAAC database, this is possible until five working days before the deadline by email to [relatiebeheer@nwo.nl](mailto:relatiebeheer@nwo.nl);
- the proposal is written in English.
