# Antarctic research and Antarctica InSync

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/antarctic-research-and-antarctica-insync

This Call for proposals invites researchers in the Netherlands to submit proposals about Antarctica, which also contribute to Antarctica InSync. Proposals should align with one or multiple of the 7 international InSync themes, and should be embedded with international research expeditions or campaigns.

## Characteristics

**Status:** In preparation  
**Closing date full application:** 16 February 2027 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)  
**Budget:** €3,000,000  
**Research programme:** Polair Programma  

## Explore

The Netherlands Polar Programme invites researchers to come with proposals for research about Antarctica, in which they also contribute to [Antarctica InSync](https://www.antarctica-insync.org/). Applications must align with one or multiple InSync themes and be embedded in international expeditions or campaigns. The Call for proposals will likely enable the application of one or two scientific positions, applied for by researchers with a fixed paid position at universities and university medical centres, and/or by researchers with a fixed or temporary position at universities of applied science and TO2 institutes.

Interested researchers are strongly advised to coordinate access to research infrastructures as timely as possible with the operators. Either via InSync, in direct contact with operators, via POLARIN’s Call for Transnational Access, or (for British Antarctic Survey facilities) the NPP Secretariat. The round will likely include a prioritisation for one proposal using Rothera and/or Dirck Gerritsz Lab.

More information is to be announced in September. The opening and publication of this Call for proposals is scheduled early October with a submission deadline mid-February 2027. The funding decision is estimated in June 2027.
