# RAAK-award 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/raak-award-2026

Every year, the Taskforce for Applied Research SIA, part of the Dutch Research Council (NWO), presents the RAAK award to the best practice-oriented research carried out within a RAAK project. The RAAK award is a widely recognized and renowned prize for practice-oriented research at universities of applied sciences.

## Characteristics

**Status:** Open calls Open for applications  
**Closing date full application:** 23 June 2026 14:00 hrs.  
**Finance type:** Applied Science  
**For specific groups:** Applied Sciences Research  
**Budget:** €45,000  
**Research programme:** RAAK-award
