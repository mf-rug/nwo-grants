# Accelerating energy transition in Dutch and Taiwanese ports

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/accelerating-energy-transition-in-dutch-and-taiwanese-ports

This call is organised in cooperation between NWO and NSTC, and aims to accelerate the energy transition of ports. Through broad research approaches, new insights should lead to scalable and world-leading low-carbon (green) port ecosystems.

## Explore

### Purpose

This NSTC-NWO call aims to accelerate the energy transition within the Taiwanese and Dutch ports, and their integrated industrial complexes, through joint research collaborations stimulating knowledge accumulation and sharing.

To ensure a holistic view of these transitions, the call focuses on the technological, economic, governmental, and social barriers of the implementation of green energy sources and technologies. Consortia are expected to use an operational and systemic approach in their research.

### Who can apply

Each consortia will consist of a side based in The Netherlands (from here on NWO-side) and one based in Taiwan( (from here on NSTC-side). Annex 7.1 in the Call for proposals states the exact requirement for consortia, per funding council.

For the NWO side of the consortium, the minimum requirements are:

- a main applicant who meets the NWO-criteria;
- a co-applicant who meets the NWO-criteria and is from a different organisation than the main applicant;
- a cooperation partner from a practitioner organisation, such as a business, (local) government, or nongovernmental organisation. Consortia are encouraged to work together with cooperation partners from a practitioner organisation, such a business, (local) government, or non-governmental organisation. The inclusion of a cooperation partner is mandatory for the NWO-side of the consortium and optional for the NSTC-side of the consortium. Please note that the mandatory cooperation partner can be based either in the Netherlands or Taiwan, depending on the needs of the consortium.

### What to apply for

Each consortia will consist of a side based in The Netherlands (from here on NWO-side) and one based in Taiwan( (from here on NSTC-side). Each side applies for funding from their own funding organisation.  Annex 7.1 in the Call for proposals states what costs will be funded by each funding organisation.

A maximum of three proposals will be awarded funding for the duration of maximum 60 months.

The budget available from NWO for this Call for proposals is € 2,1 million. Per project, the NWO-side can apply for a grant with a maximum of € 700,000.00. The applicant and co-applicants can include costs for personnel, materials, knowledge utilisation, and project management.

The budget available from NSTC for this Call for proposals is NT$45 million. Per project, the NSTC-side can apply NT$3,000,000 per year, with a maximum of 60 months.

### When to apply

Applicants should be aware of four submission deadlines in this Call for proposals:

1. The deadline for submitting statements of intent is **29 October 2026**, before 14:00:00 CET, at NWO on behalf of the entire consortium. On the 30th of October 2026 the application for full proposals will open in ISAAC.
2. The deadline for applicants whose (foreign) organisations need to be assessed by NWO (see the NWO regulations in Annex 7.1 of the Call for proposals) is 21 January 2027, before 14:00:00 CET.
3. The deadline for submitting full proposals at NWO is **4 February 2027**, before 14:00:00 CET.
4. The deadline for submitting full proposals at NSTC (which is the same full proposal submitted to NWO) is 4 March 2027 (this is due to Chinese New Year holiday period)

### Assessment

### Criteria

The applications submitted within this Call for proposals will be substantially assessed on the basis of the following criteria:

1. Quality of the research
2. Potential for societal impact
3. Quality of the consortium
The criteria carry equal weight and each count for one-third of the final assessment.

### Procedure

The Executive Board from NWO and NSTC will appoint an independent assessment committee. It is the assessment committee’s task to assess the proposals received against the assessment criteria. Each proposal is assessed on its own merits.

In this assessment procedure, there will be no external referees consulted. Instead selected committee members will provide a pre-assessment, after which each application can submit a rebuttal. During the final assessment meeting, the committee will form a final assessment taking into account the full applications, preassessments and rebuttals. Based on their advice, the Executive board of NWO and NSTC will make the final decision.

## Prepare

Applications must be submitted through the ISAAC application and reporting system. If you do not have an ISAAC account yet, then please create one at least one workday in advance. More information about writing a proposal and which forms you need to use can be found in ISAAC. Please download and use the forms in ISAAC. Below you will find examples of the required forms.

### Write proposal

Applicants are advised to carefully read the Call for Proposals and other relevant information. If they do not yet have contacts with researchers in Taiwan, participation in the online matchmaking event is recommended, but not mandatory. In addition to the quality of the research and the consortium, applications will also be assessed on their potential for impact. To support the preparation of their proposal, applicants can participate free of charge in the online training course “**NWO Impact – Working with an Impact Plan**.” This training is particularly suitable for completing together with part of the consortium, helping to ensure that impact is given a clear and prominent place within the proposal. Applicants are therefore encouraged to identify and engage relevant cooperation partners and stakeholders within the target group of the call at an early stage.

### Mandatory consortium-building activities

On 28 August, NWO and NSTC are organising a matchmaking event for the upcoming call ‘Accelerating Energy Transition in Dutch and Taiwanese Ports’. During the session, the key information from the Call for proposals will be briefly explained, and there will be an opportunity to meet other researchers. This matchmaking is NOT mandatory, but both councils want to facilitate researchers who do not possess a (wide) network yet. The online session will start with a brief introduction of the Call and will then mainly focus on matchmaking break out sessions. For the participation of the matchmaking, an adequate level of English is needed as there will be not translations possible.

### Fill in forms

Applicants represented by NWO must submit their application and accompanying documents through ISAAC, NWO’s online application and reporting system. NWO advises applicants to register in ISAAC and familiarise themselves with the system well in advance of the submission deadline, at least five working days before the deadline.

Within ISAAC, applicants can convert their statement of intent into a full proposal. A full proposal cannot be submitted without a prior statement of intent.

The following documents must be uploaded:

- Budget form (Excel file);
- Knowledge Security Declaration (see the NWO section in Annex 7.1 of the Call for Proposals) (PDF);
- Letters of Commitment from the institutions of all main applicants and co-applicants (PDF);
- Declarations of Support from all cooperation partners (without co-funding) (PDF).
Optional:

- Declarations of Co-funding from all co-funding partners (PDF).
**PLEASE NOTE:** the Taiwanese main applicant is expected to submit documents to NSTC.
