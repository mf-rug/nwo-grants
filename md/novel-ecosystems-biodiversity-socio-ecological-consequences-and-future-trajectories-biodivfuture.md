# Novel ecosystems: biodiversity, socio-ecological consequences and future trajectories (BiodivFuture)

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/novel-ecosystems-biodiversity-socio-ecological-consequences-and-future-trajectories-biodivfuture

## Explore

### Purpose

The European Partnership *Rescuing Biodiversity to Safeguard Life on Earth* (Biodiversa+) is a collaboration between research and innovation funders, aimed at reversing the loss of biodiversity in Europe from 2030 onwards. Biodiversa+ provides an overarching platform connecting national, local and European research and innovation programs and combines resources, in line with the *European Green Deal* and the *EU Biodiversity Strategy for 2030*. Biodiversa+ is co-funded by the European Commission.

The sixth joint call of the partnership focuses on the topic *Novel ecosystems: biodiversity, socio-ecological consequences and future trajectorie*s (BiodivFuture). As ecosystems become ever more shaped by human influence and global change, BiodivFuture aims to fund research to better understand ongoing transformations, the risks, opportunities and trade-offs they entail, and how knowledge can help foster resilient, interconnected, diverse and well-functioning ecosystems. This call covers all realms (terrestrial, freshwater, marine and coastal areas) across the globe.

###### The Call will focus on three themes:

**A. Novel ecosystem functioning**

Understanding the functioning of novel ecosystems and their links to socio-cultural and socio-economic dynamics.

**B. Pathways to novel ecosystems**

Studying pathways to novel ecosystems and associated socio-ecological dynamics under uncertainty and changing conditions.

**C. Biodiversity benefits and equity in the context of novel ecosystems**

Exploring how biodiversity benefits, costs and trade-offs are shared across contexts, communities and generation

### Who can apply

This Call is open to transnational consortia of at least three partners requesting funding from three participating countries. At least two of these must be EU Member States or associated countries. Organisations which are not eligible for funding from a participating funder may join a consortium as self-funded partners, in addition to the minimum composition of the consortium.

On the Biodiversa+ website, you can find a list of participating countries and search for partners using the partner search tool.

Researchers from research organisations in the Kingdom of the Netherlands can submit applications to NWO. See the national requirements for NWO (Annex A) and the Call for proposals for further information.

An application for funding from NWO may consider the following roles:

- **National main applicant (mandatory)**: the national applicant who leads the application to NWO and is the foreseen national project leader, in case a project is granted.
- **National co-applicant (optional)**: national applicants in addition to the national main applicant with an active role and responsibility in realising the project and requesting funding from NWO. Please note: national co-applicants are ‘Team members’ of the national main applicant in the Biodiversa+ application form.
A researcher may only request NWO funding for one project (part of a transnational consortium) in this Call for proposals, either as national main applicant or national co-applicant within an application for NWO funding.

### Assessment

### Criteria

Pre-proposals will be reviewed on the basis of the following three criteria:

- Fit to the scope of the call
- Novelty of the research
- Impact
Full proposals will be reviewed on the basis of the following three criteria:

- Excellence;
- Quality and efficiency of the implementation;
- Impact.
Please see the full Biodiversa+ Document 6: Assessment criteria for the criteria including the sub-criteria.

### Procedure

The assessment in this Call will be done in two stages. In the first stage consortia are invited to submit a pre-proposal. Only consortia that were successful in the first stage can submit a full proposal. Eligible proposals – both in the first and in the second stage – will be assessed by an independent Evaluation Committee consisting both of scientific and policy/management experts. The proposals will be ranked based on the criteria in the Call for proposals. Based on the ranking and the budget available, the national and regional funding agencies will take the funding decision. At NWO the Executive Board will take this decision.
