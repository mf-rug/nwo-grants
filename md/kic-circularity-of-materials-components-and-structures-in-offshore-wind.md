# KIC - Circularity of materials, components and structures in offshore wind

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/kic-circularity-of-materials-components-and-structures-in-offshore-wind

This KIC-call focuses on knowledge development that contributes to extending the service life of offshore wind turbines and the high-value reuse of materials and components following decommissioning. The programme calls for knowledge development on material degradation and failure mechanisms, as well as on design and material innovations to achieve a longer service life and greater circularity. In addition, the development of strategies for the reuse, refurbishment and recycling of offshore wind turbines is central to the programme.

Technological, economic, legal and organisational perspectives are explicitly considered in conjunction with one another to support the implementation of circular solutions.
