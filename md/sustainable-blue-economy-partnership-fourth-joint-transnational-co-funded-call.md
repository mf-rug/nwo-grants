# Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership: Fourth Joint Transnational Co-funded Call

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/sustainable-blue-economy-partnership-fourth-joint-transnational-co-funded-call

Building a sustainable and innovative Blue Economy: mastering digital opportunities and fostering resilience  

Experienced researchers can apply for funding for international research projects that contribute to finding solutions towards a climate-neutral, sustainable, productive and competitive blue economy in Europe. This is the fourth call within the European co-funded partnership Sustainable Blue Economy Partnership (SBEP) under Horizon Europe.

## Explore

### Purpose

The SBEP call 2026 for proposals pools national and regional funding for the development of knowledge and innovation that boost the transition to a climate neutral, sustainable and productive Blue Economy.

This SBEP Call 2026 co-funded call aims to support transnational research and innovation addressing the priority areas below:

1. Transitioning the blue economy sectors for sustainability and strategic autonomy
2. Managing sea-uses by advancing digital and ecosystem-based approaches
3. Promoting sustainable blue bioresources through innovation in marine production and harvesting
4. Building resilience in coastal communities with infrastructure and businesses
The proposals are strongly recommended to consider a minimum of two EU sea basins which are: the Atlantic Ocean, Baltic Sea, Black Sea, Mediterranean Sea, and North Sea. Proposals can target European regional seas such as Adriatic Sea, Aegean Sea, the Arctic, Barents Sea, Celtic Sea, etc. but they must be from a minimum of 2 different EU sea basins.

Applications are submitted to the joint call secretariat via the European EPSS system. For more information, see: [Funding opportunities | Bluepartnership](https://bluepartnership.eu/funding-opportunities). For Dutch participation in applications, submission of national documents is mandatory; see under ‘Preparation’.

### Who can apply

Applications need to be submitted by international research consortia including teams from at least three countries participating in the call. For more details refer to the call for proposals.

For Dutch participation in proposals, funding can be requested at NWO by professors, associate professors, assistant professors, professors of applied sciences, and other researchers holding a comparable appointment at universities, universities of applied sciences, university medical centres, KNAW and NWO institutes, TO2 institutes, and the research institutes listed in Annex B of the Call for Proposals: The National/Regional Regulations, The Netherlands.

An application for NWO funding has one national main applicant, responsible for scientific and financial management. National co-applicants within an application for NWO funding are also allowed.

Applicants may be involved in a maximum of one proposal as national main applicant and/or national co-applicant (as part of a European consortium).

More information on the eligibility criteria, including the requirements from other countries, can be found in the 4th SBEP Call 2026 Documents.

### Consortium building

To find European partners you can use the [partner search platform](https://proposals.etag.ee/sustainable-blue/2026/partner-search). This is not obligatory.

### What to apply for

A total of €1,400,000 is made available for Dutch participation in the 4th SBEP Call 2026.

Per application, a maximum of €400,000 can be requested from NWO if there is one national main applicant, and up to €600,000 if there is both a national main applicant and a co-applicant. The application concerns a project with a maximum duration of 3 years.

There is no limit on the size of the international consortium (neither financially nor in terms of the number of partners); the national funding organisations only impose a limit on the amount that can be requested nationally by each partner.

The 4th SBEP Call 2026 also allows for the inclusion of 'self-funded partners' in the international consortium.

Eligible costs for NWO funding can be found in the SBEP Call 2026, Annex B: National Contact Points and National/Regional regulations. Please note: PhD positions cannot be applied for in this call, due to the maximum project duration of 3 years. Moreover, NWO funds project-related costs. Therefore overhead costs are not eligible for NWO funding.

### Assessment

### Criteria

Eligible Pre-proposals will be reviewed on the basis of the following two criteria:

- Excellence
- Impact
Eligible Full proposals will be reviewed on the basis of the following three criteria:

- Excellence
- Impact
- Quality and efficiency of the implementation
Please see the full SBEP Call 2026 Documents for the criteria including the sub-criteria.

### Procedure

The assessment in this call will be done in two stages. In the first stage, consortia are invited to submit a pre-proposal. Only consortia that were successful in the first stage can submit a full proposal. Eligible proposals – both in the first and in the second stage – will be assessed by an independent Evaluation Committee consisting of experts from relevant scientific fields. The proposals will be ranked based on the criteria in the call text. Based on the ranking and the budget available, the national and regional funding agencies will take the funding decision. At NWO the Executive Board will take this decision.
