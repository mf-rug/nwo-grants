# KIN Knowledge in Action - Watertransition Twente

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/kin-knowledge-in-action-watertransition-twente

The Knowledge-in-Action grant brings together (action)researchers and societal actors—such as entrepreneurs, practice pioneers, creative professionals and entrepreneurial policy-makers—to work jointly in co-creation on just climate transitions. In this Knowledge-in-Action grant, one urgent regional question is central: How can the water transition in Twente be designed in such a way that – given increasing drought, historical choices and other societal transitions – a resilient and equitable water system emerges?

## Explore

### Purpose

The Knowledge-in-Action grant mobilizes science and practice for just climate transitions through learning by doing and doing by learning. Knowledge institutions and societal organizations can submit joint applications for transformative, experimental intervention projects to jointly explore what the desired transition might look like: which solution directions, investigative questions, and transition learning objectives can accelerate the desired transition?

Two sub-themes are central:

- System choices: What are the critical substantive breakthroughs in the transition to a resilient future in which water and soil are leading factors?
- Transition, governance, and organization: How do we organize joint change in a long-term and risk-averse system?

### Who can apply

Employees of knowledge institutions and societal organizations established in the Kingdom of the Netherlands can submit an application.

### Consortium building

The collaboration between knowledge and practice is central to this call. The project partners (=applicants) must therefore consist of at least one scientific knowledge institution, including universities of applied sciences, and at least one societal organization/company.

### What to apply for

The maximum available budget per project is € 50.000.

### When to apply

- The deadline for applications is **Tuesday 22 September 2026 at 14:00:00 CEST**

### Assessment

### Criteria

Applications are assessed on four main criteria;

- Relevance to the Knowledge-in-Action agenda
- Quality of the application
- Co-creation & knowledge development
- Appropriateness and feasibility of activities and budget

### Procedure

The procedure consists of submission, assessment of admissibility, preliminary advice by the evaluation committee, a committee meeting, and decision-making. If there are more applications with a positive advice than there is available budget, an additional selection takes place. Applications are assessed on four main criteria—relevance, quality, co-creation & knowledge development, and feasibility—where each criterion must score sufficiently. The evaluation committee consists of academic and practical experts.
