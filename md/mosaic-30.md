# Mosaic 3.0

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/mosaic-30

The Mosaic 3.0 PhD scholarship programme encourages graduates with a non-Western migration background—a group underrepresented in the Dutch scientific community—to pursue a career in science.

## Characteristics

**Status:** In preparation  
**Closing date for pre-proposals:** 3 November 2026 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)  
**Closing date full application:** 4 May 2027 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)  
**Budget:** €3,600,000  
**Research programme:** Mozaiek 3.0
