# FutureFoodS Call 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/futurefoods-call-2026

The research and innovation funders within the European Partnership FutureFoodS are jointly launching a transnational call for proposals, in which NWO intends to make funding available to applicants in the Kingdom of the Netherlands. Taskforce for Applied Research SIA and the Ministry of Agriculture, Fisheries, Food Security and Nature (LVVN) will also make funding available under separate requirements. This call focuses on the development of sustainable, healthy and safe food practices and the sustainable governance of food systems and food consumption in Europe.

Definitive participation in the call and the national requirements will, at the latest, be confirmed with the transnational launch of the call for proposals, which is expected early November 2026. Up-to-date information is available on the FutureFoodS website.
