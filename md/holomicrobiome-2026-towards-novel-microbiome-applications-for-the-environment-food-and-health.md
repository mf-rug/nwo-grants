# Holomicrobiome 2026 - Towards novel microbiome applications for the environment, food and health

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/holomicrobiome-2026-towards-novel-microbiome-applications-for-the-environment-food-and-health

Microbiomes, communities of organisms such as bacteria, fungi and viruses, greatly impact the health of humans, animals, plants and the environment. This call invites researchers to create consortia for research and development of innovative applications of microbiomes in areas such as agriculture, livestock farming, food production and health care.

## Explore

### Purpose

Our society faces major challenges in the areas of climate, environmental quality, circularity, and animal and public health. Microbiomes have the potential to offer sustainable solutions, for example by reducing harmful emissions in agriculture and livestock farming, and by strengthening the resilience and health of humans and animals. Microbiomes can also play a role in preventing and combating antimicrobial resistance (AMR) or improving the quality of surface water. The knowledge gained through this programme therefore not only leads to the development of innovative solutions, but also helps to tackle these societal challenges.

### Who can apply

Full, associate and assistant professors and other researchers with a comparable position may submit an application if they have a position at one of the Dutch knowledge organisations as mentioned in the Call for proposals. Applications are submitted by a main applicant and one or more co-applicants on behalf of the consortium. An important condition is that the main applicant (or their replacement) has participated in the matchmaking. Staff members of the following organisations may act as co-applicants:

1. the research organisations listed under ‘main applicant’ in the Call for proposals;
2. Dutch research organisations that meet the eligibility criteria for organisations based in the Netherlands;
3. foreign research organisations that meet the eligibility criteria for organisations based abroad;
4. companies and other public and private organisations. The description of who may act as an applicant in the Call for proposals is decisive.

### Consortium building

A consortium should consist of at least a main applicant, co-applicant, and two co-funders. In case of Flagship projects, at least two different research institutes need to be involved. Collaboration partners are not obligatory, but can be part of the consortium.

### What to apply for

The subsidy ceiling for this Call for proposals is € 26,000,000. In this Call for proposals there are two categories for which a grant can be applied for: ‘Flagship projects’ and ‘Use Case projects’. For each of these categories, a maximum € 13,000,000 is available.

#### Category 1

Flagship projects Per project, a minimum of € 1,500,000 and maximum € 3,250,000 grant can be applied for. Maximum duration: 60 months. Each application requires at least 15% of the total project costs to be ‘Non-NWO funding’.

#### Category 2

Use case projects Per project, a minimum of € 500,000 and maximum € 1,500,000 grant can be applied for. Maximum duration: 36 months. Each application requires at least 30% of the total project costs to be ‘Non-NWO funding’.

#### Both categories

A minimum of 50% of the applied for grant should go to the research organisations listed as ‘main applicant’ in the call for proposals. The main applicant and co-applicants can apply for personnel, material, knowledge utilization and (only Flagship projects) investments.

### Assessment

### Criteria

#### Assessment category 1

Comply with the Framework for State aid for research, development and innovation (RDI Framework) For research organisations: the assessment committee assesses to what extent the activities denoted as noneconomic in nature qualify as independent scientific research by the research organisation in the partnership, aimed at more knowledge and better insight.

#### Assessment category 2

- Criterium 1. Fit of the project in the selected category (10%),
- Criterium 2. Quality of the consortium (30%),
- Criterium 3. Scientific and/or societal impact (30%),
- Criterium 4. Quality of the research plan (30%).

### Procedure

Participation in the matchmaking is obligatory for main applicants. Admissible proposals are assessed by independent external experts. Applicants will be invited for interview to elucidate their proposal to the assessment committee. If more than twice the amount of proposals is submitted in a category than can be awarded, this Call for proposals will include an interview selection. The assessment committee prepares a ranking and advices the board of domain AES about the quality of the applications. The board of domain AES decides, based on the advice of the committee and the available funds, on whether or not to award funding.

### Write proposal

The application makes use of the correct application form (i.e. either for a Flagship project ór a Use Case project), and should clarify which problem the project addresses (i.e. either a Challenge for a Flagship project or development of a microbiome product in the Use Case projects). The application also describes the goals, hypotheses, research methods and scientific feasibility of the project. In addition, at leaste two cofunders must be mentioned, as well as any collaboration partners. The application should also describe how the research will lead to scientific and/ or societal impact.
