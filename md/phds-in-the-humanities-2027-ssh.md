# PhDs in the Humanities 2027 SSH

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/phds-in-the-humanities-2027-ssh

The aim of the programme PhDs in the Humanities is to provide research talent with the opportunity to carry out an independent PhD project.

## Characteristics

**Status:** Upcoming  
**Closing date full application:** 16 February 2027 14:00 hrs.  
**Budget:** €5,173,000  
**Research programme:** PhDs in the Humanities
