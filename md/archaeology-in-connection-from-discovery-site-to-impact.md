# Archaeology in Connection: from discovery site to impact

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/archaeology-in-connection-from-discovery-site-to-impact

This Call for proposals invites research organisations, municipalities, provinces, archaeological research firms, public bodies, and other societal partners to form transdisciplinary research consortia. The objective is to generate and share knowledge and insights that contribute to enhancing the depth and quality of archaeological research in the Netherlands. The program seeks innovative insights and approaches that foster collaboration across the entire research chain, enabling participants to learn effectively from on another. Consequently, these transdisciplinary consortia operate across the full knowledge chain: projects encompass fundamental, applied and practice-oriented research, and - through the active contribution of vairous parties - explicitly adress the knowledge needs of the diverse stakeholders involved in the field of archaeology.
