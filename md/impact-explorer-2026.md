# Impact Explorer 2026

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/impact-explorer-2026

Impact Explorer is a small top-up, for ongoing research-projects in the open competition or talent line, to explore an unforeseen opportunity for impact in close collaboration with an Impact Partner.

## Characteristics

**Status:** Upcoming  
**Closing date full application:** 5 October 2027 14:00 hrs.  
**Finance type:** Curiosity Driven Research  
**For specific groups:** Collaboration with social partners Knowledge Utilisation  
**Budget:** €1,450,000  
**Research programme:** Impact Explorer  

## Explore

### Purpose

The purpose of this grant, identically to the earlier Impact Explorer round, is to explore whether and how an unexpected research outcome, or an unexpected application of a research outcome, from curiosity-driven research can lead to societal impact. These are opportunities and ideas that were not anticipated in advance when the project plan was made, but have emerged during the lifetime of a project.

Only explorations based on research arising from the Talent Programme and the Open Competition of both NWO and ZonMw are eligible for funding.

### Who can apply

Researchers with a running project in one of NWO or ZonMw's open competitions or talent line programmes can submit. For each ongoing project, only one proposal may be assessed in the Impact Explorer programme. Impact Explorer applications should be submitted with at least one Impact Partner. For specific conditions, see chapter 3.2 in the call.

### What to apply for

Researchers can apply for a minimum of 20,000 and a maximum of 35,000 euro. It is not possible to use it to extend or fund appointments of temporary academic staff. The maximum duration of the proposed project is 12 months. For more details, see chapter 3.4 in the call.

### When to apply

- Applications may be submitted to NWO on a continuous basis from Tuesday 6 October 2026 up to and including **Tuesday 5 October 2027 before 14:00:00 CEST**.

### Assessment

### Criteria

An application will be assessed individually for:

1. Alignment with the programme’s objectives
2. Quality and feasibility of the project plan
3. Quality of the team
See chapter 4.1 in the call for details.

### Procedure

Each application is assessed individually whether it is of sufficient quality to be granted. This is done by means of a jury without referees. If an application scores 3.4 (very good) or better on each of the criteria, it will be approved provided there is sufficient budget available.

Applications are collected for review at eight times during the runtime of the programme. Applications are granted until the budget ceiling is reached. See chapter 3.1 in the call for an overview of the collection moments.

PLEASE NOTE: Proposals are not in competition with one another. Decisions on granting and rejecting proposals are made on the basis of the proposals’ scores and on a first come, first served basis. If the available budget is insufficient to allocate all proposals eligible for award based on the outcome of the assessment procedure, the order of entry will be decisive. For elaboration, see chapter 4.2 in the call.

### More information

The main changes of this extension from the earlier Impact Explorer round 2023 are:

- Submission requirements: one substantive submission per ongoing project is allowed to the Impact Explorer programme (section 3.2);
- Grant ceiling and maximum grant: The total grant ceiling for this Call for proposals is €1,450,000. The minimum grant per project is €20,000 and the maximum is €35,000 (section 3.4);
- Assessment criteria: Criterion 1b now requires, alongside the societal relevance of the exploration, also appropriate consideration of risks of undesirable societal impact arising from the exploration (section 4.1).

## Prepare

Applications must be submitted through the Isaac application and reporting system. If you do not have an Isaac account yet, then please create one at least one workday in advance. More information about writing a proposal and which forms you need to use can be found in Isaac. Please download and use the forms in Isaac. Below you will find examples of the required forms.

### Write proposal

Fill in the application form. There is relatively limited space to elaborate in details. The jury is not composed of experts from your own field, so keep this in mind when writing the application. In addition, make sure you look for an Impact Partner as early in the process as possible.

### Fill in forms

You need to complete both the application form and obtain a letter of support from the Impact Partner. In the situation that your contract ends during the project, a statement appointment and project supervision is also required.
