# Impact Explorer – Exploring opportunities for societal impact

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/impact-explorer-exploring-opportunities-for-societal-impact

The aim of this top-up grant is to explore whether and how an unexpected research outcome, or an unexpected application of a research outcome, arising from curiosity-driven research can lead to societal impact. Only projects based on research arising from the Talent Programme and the Open Competition of both NWO and ZonMw are eligible for funding.

## Characteristics

**Status:** In preparation  
**Budget:** €1,450,000  
**Research programme:** Impact Explorer
