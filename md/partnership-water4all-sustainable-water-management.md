# Partnership Water4All - Sustainable Water Management

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/partnership-water4all-sustainable-water-management

The Water4All Partnership (Water security for the planet) is a European partnership under Horizon Europe. The partnership focuses on addressing challenges relating to freshwater, particularly in the context of climate change.

## Characteristics

**Status:** In preparation  
**Research programme:** WATER4ALL – Water Security for the Planet  

## Explore

The research and innovation funding bodies in Water4All are jointly launching a transnational call for proposals, in which NWO intends to make funding available to applicants in the Kingdom of the Netherlands. NWO participates in this call on Sustainable Water Management, with the following four themes:

- Integrated monitoring and assessment for sustainable water management,
- Nature-based approaches for resilient water management at the scale of hydrological and hydrogeological systems,
- Smart, secure and adaptive water infrastructures for sustainable water management,
- Resilient water systems through systemic change, policy coherence and inclusive governance.
Final confirmation of participation and the national conditions will be provided no later than the launch of the call for proposals in September 2026. Up-to-date information is available on t[he Water4All website](//www.water4all-partnership.eu/joint-activities/water4all-2026-joint-transnational-call-pre-announcement).
