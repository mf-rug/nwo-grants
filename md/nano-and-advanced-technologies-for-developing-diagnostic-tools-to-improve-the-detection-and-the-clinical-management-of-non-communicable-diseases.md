# Nano and advanced technologies for developing Diagnostic tools to improve the detection and the clinical management of non-communicable diseases

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/nano-and-advanced-technologies-for-developing-diagnostic-tools-to-improve-the-detection-and-the-clinical-management-of-non-communicable-diseases

ERA4Health is a European Partnership under Horizon Europe and aims for funding international research in the areas of cardiovascular disease, nanomedicine, and nutrition, health and prevention.

## Characteristics

**Status:** In preparation  
**Budget:** €3,259,521  
**Research programme:** ERA for Health (ERA4Health)  

## Explore

The research funders of ERA4Health will open a 10th Joint Transnational Call for Proposals. NWO expects to participate and to make funding available for applicants of the Kingdom of The Netherlands. The call topic will be 'Nano and advanced technologies for developing Diagnostic tools to improve the detection and the clinical management of non-communicable diseases'.

Participation in the call and the national criteria will be confirmed on 17 November 2026 at the transnational launch of the Call for Proposals. Current information is available on [the ERA4Health website](https://era4health.eu/calls/pre_NanoDiagNCD2027.php) .

**Important deadlines**

- 17 November 2026 : Publication of the call
- 19 November 2026 : Webinar info day
- 19 January 2027 : Deadline preproposals
- 9 June 2027 : Deadline full proposals
