# Perspectief round 2026-2027

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/perspectief-round-2026-2027

WO Domain AES invites researchers and users (companies, societal organisations) to jointly submit new, challenging research projects within the application-oriented and technical sciences that generate economic and societal impact in thematic areas relevant to the Netherlands.

## Characteristics

**Status:** In preparation  
**Closing date for letter of intent:** 6 October 2026 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)  
**Closing date for pre-proposals:** 24 November 2026 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)  
**Closing date full application:** 25 May 2027 14:00 hrs. (s.t.c.)  
**Research programme:** 2026-2027
