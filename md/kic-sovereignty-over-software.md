# KIC Sovereignty over Software

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/kic-sovereignty-over-software

Reliable software forms the cornerstone of digitalisation. The ability of organisations to maintain control over their software is essential for the continuity of service provision and for their ability to adapt to technical changes, regulatory requirements, and the needs of society.

## Characteristics

## Explore

The aim of this call is to strengthen organizations’ sovereignty over software by developing knowledge and methodologies that help to understand and manage the growing complexity of software. The programme focuses both on new knowledge and methods for software development and on gaining insight into how organisations develop, manage and use software. This requires a transdisciplinary approach, in which knowledge institutions, industry and social organisations collaborate on innovative solutions that find their way into practice.
