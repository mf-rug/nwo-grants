# Isaac Newton Group Telescopes 27a

**Source:** https://www.nwo.nl/en/calls/isaac-newton-group-telescopes-27a

Astronomers in the Netherlands are invited to apply for observation time at the Isaac Newton Group of Telescopes on La Palma (Canary Islands, Spain).

## Characteristics

**Status:** Open for applications  
**Closing date full application:** 1 October 2026 14:00 hrs.  
**Research programme:** Observation time on the Isaac Newton Group of Telescopes  

## Explore

### Purpose

Astronomers can apply for observing time at the Isaac Newton Group of Telescopes (ING) on La Palma. The ING consists of the 4.2 m William Herschel Telescope (WHT) and the 2.5 m Isaac Newton Telescope (INT).

### Who can apply

Researchers in astronomy with a Dutch affiliation.

### What to apply for

- Dutch observing time at WHT for observations with WEAVE to be carried out between 1 May 2027 and 31 October 2027.

### WEAVE

Proposals are invited for observations with WEAVE at WHT. In semester 2027A all three WEAVE observing modes are offered: MOS (multi-object spectroscopy), LIFU (large integral-field unit) and mini IFUs (mini integral-field units). MOS and mini-IFUs are available on a shared risk basis. Target of Opportunity and non sidereal observations can be applied for with WEAVE LIFU.

The effective available Dutch observing time for open time proposals with WEAVE in semester 2027A is expected to be approximately 53 hours.

### Note regarding HARPS3

HARPS3 is not offered for new proposals in the 2027A semester.

For more information, see [https://www.ing.iac.es/astronomy/instr.html](https://www.ing.iac.es/astronomy/instr.html).

### When to apply

The next deadline is 1 October 2026. This is the deadline for:

- observing time with WEAVE@WHT to be carried out between 1 May 2027 and 31 October 2027.
Note: The ING issues two calls for proposals per year. The associated deadlines for Dutch open time proposals are yearly expected in the second half of March and in the second half of September.

### Assessment

### **Criteria**

Proposals will be assessed by the ING Programme Committee of NWO against the scientific quality of the proposed project. Because proposals for observing time come from many different fields of research, we recommend you to keep in mind that not all members of the Programme Committee are experts in your specific area of research. Please include the necessary background information and clearly formulate the relevance of your science question and how the requested data will contribute to answering this science question.

### **Procedure**

The Programme Committee of NWO assesses proposals for Dutch observing time. International proposals are assessed by a different committee.

In general the telescope organisation will inform you on the success of your proposal approximately three months after submission. Around the same time, you will also receive feedback from the Programme Committee.
